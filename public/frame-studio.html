Frame Studio Walkthrough

Follow this flow from upload to final save.

Upload video: Click the drop area and select your clip.
↓
Choose a template: Start with a preset and let the controls fill in for you.
↓
Choose format: Pick TikTok, Reels, YouTube, or keep original ratio.
↓
Style your frame: Set background, frame, text, captions, and stickers.
↓
Move text around: Drag headline, timed captions, or watermark directly on the canvas.
↓
Preview audio: In Audio tab choose Keep, Mix, or Replace, then press Play.
↓
Trim and quality: Set start/end points and export resolution.
↓
Render and save: Click Render & preview, then Save video (5 credits).
